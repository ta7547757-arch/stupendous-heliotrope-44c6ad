BINTAAH • YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. Learn how Bintaah collects, uses, and protects your information.

✧ Last Updated: October 2, 2026
A NOTE FROM BINTAAH
✦

At Bintaah by Choynika, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information when you visit our website or place an order with us.

Your Information

Privacy, handled with care.

01
PERSONAL INFORMATION
✦

Information We Collect

When you use our website or place an order, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Order details
  • Payment or Cash on Delivery information
  • Account information, if you create an account
  • Information provided when contacting customer care
02
OUR PURPOSE
✦

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Contact you regarding your order
  • Verify orders when necessary
  • Provide customer support
  • Manage your account
  • Send important order-related updates
  • Improve our website and services
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
03
YOUR ORDER
✦

Order Information

When you place an order with Bintaah, we need certain information to process and deliver your order.

Your name, phone number, and delivery address may be shared with delivery or courier partners when necessary to complete your delivery.

We only share information that is reasonably necessary to provide the requested service.

04
PAYMENT
✦

Payment Information

Bintaah currently offers Cash on Delivery and may introduce additional payment methods in the future.

We do not intentionally store sensitive payment information such as card numbers or security codes on our website unless required by a secure payment service provider.

05
WEBSITE EXPERIENCE
✦

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality and your browsing experience.

  • Remember your preferences
  • Keep items in your shopping cart
  • Improve website performance
  • Understand how visitors use our website
06
SECURITY
✦

Protecting Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no online system can guarantee complete security of information transmitted over the internet.

07
TRANSPARENCY
✦

Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Limited information may be shared with trusted service providers when necessary, including:

  • Delivery and courier partners
  • Website and hosting providers
  • Payment service providers
  • Technical or customer-support providers
08
COMMUNICATION
✦

Marketing Communications

If you choose to receive promotional communications from Bintaah, we may send information about new collections, offers, or updates.

You may request to stop receiving promotional communications at any time by contacting us.

09
EXTERNAL SERVICES
✦

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Bintaah is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

10
YOUR RIGHTS
✦

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Ask questions about how your information is used
11
UPDATES
✦

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website, or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

12
WE ARE HERE FOR YOU
✦

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your information, we are happy to assist you.

You may contact Bintaah through our Customer Care or the contact information provided on our website.
