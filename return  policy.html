Return Policy

Returns made with care.

YOUR PURCHASE

Our return policy, simply explained.

At Bintaah, we want every piece you receive to feel beautiful, comfortable, and right for you.

01
ELIGIBILITY ✦

When Can You Return an Item?

We accept return requests for eligible products that are received damaged, defective, incorrect, or significantly different from the product description.

The item must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and returned in its original condition and packaging.

02
RETURN WINDOW ✦

Return Request Period

If you receive an eligible item that you wish to return, please contact us within 3 days of receiving your order.

Return requests submitted after this period may not be accepted.

03
ITEM CONDITION ✦

Condition of Returned Items

To be eligible for a return, the product must be in the same condition in which you received it.

  • Unused and unworn
  • Unwashed
  • Free from perfume, makeup, or stains
  • Original tags should remain attached
  • Original packaging should be preserved
04
WRONG ITEM ✦

Incorrect Product Received

If you receive a product different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible.

After verification, we will guide you through the return or replacement process.

05
PRODUCT CONDITION ✦

Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or has a manufacturing defect, please contact our customer care team immediately after receiving the order.

We may request photographs or other information to verify the issue before approving the return.

06
EXCLUSIONS ✦

Items That Cannot Be Returned

For hygiene and product-quality reasons, certain items may not be eligible for return once they have been used or worn.

  • Used or worn products
  • Washed products
  • Products with stains or strong fragrance
  • Items damaged after delivery due to misuse
  • Items returned without original condition
07
EXCHANGE ✦

Exchange Requests

Exchanges may be considered depending on product availability and the reason for the request.

Please contact our customer care team before sending any item back.

08
RETURN DELIVERY ✦

Return Shipping

Return delivery arrangements depend on the reason for the return.

If the return is due to an incorrect, damaged, or defective item received from Bintaah, we will provide further instructions regarding the return.

09
REFUND ✦

Refunds

Once a returned product is received and inspected, we will inform you whether the return has been approved.

Any approved refund will be handled according to the applicable payment or order arrangement.

10
CASH ON DELIVERY ✦

Cash on Delivery Orders

For Cash on Delivery orders, refund arrangements may require additional information from the customer.

Our customer care team will explain the applicable process when a refund is approved.

11
RETURN REQUEST ✦

How to Request a Return

To request a return, please contact Bintaah Customer Care with your order details and a clear explanation of the issue.

We may ask for photographs or additional information before confirming the return.

12
WE ARE HERE FOR YOU ✦

Need Help?

If you are unsure whether your order qualifies for a return, please contact our Customer Care team before sending anything back.

We are always happy to help make your Bintaah shopping experience simple and comfortable.
